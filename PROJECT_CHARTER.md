# EKHC OS — Project Charter (A1 Draft)

**Status:** Draft for Owner review; not approved or frozen  
**Stage:** A1 — Assessment, specification, and architecture

## 1. Purpose

Establish a secure, integrated, Amharic-first and multilingual digital operating platform to support the Ethiopian Kale Heywot Church's governance, organizational administration, membership, ministries, communication, education, and operational workflows across Ethiopia.

## 2. Project independence

EKHC OS is an independent project and repository. It must not inherit or become coupled to ARNA, MASES, MaaleNex, or IROKA. Other systems may be examined only where explicitly authorized and where doing so does not compromise this independence.

## 3. Intended outcomes

- Consistent, governed digital workflows across authorized church organizational levels.
- Reliable membership and organizational records with clear stewardship and access boundaries.
- Amharic-first user experience and support for approved additional languages.
- Usable web and mobile experiences, including offline-capable workflows where validated.
- Traceable decisions, auditable administrative actions, and secure information handling.
- A maintainable architecture that can evolve without premature distributed-system complexity.

These outcomes are goals for A1 validation, not a declaration that features are approved for implementation.

## 4. Delivery principles

1. Owner-governed, explicit stage approvals.
2. Evidence-based requirements and source traceability.
3. Privacy, least privilege, and organization-scoped access by design.
4. Offline resilience with controlled synchronization and conflict handling.
5. Accessibility, low-bandwidth operation, and mobile usability.
6. No production implementation before A1 approval and A2 reconciliation authorization.
7. No merge, deployment, signing, or publication without the relevant separate approval.

## 5. Stage gates

- **A1:** assessment, formal specification, architecture proposal, risk register, and baseline for review.
- **A2:** read-only reconciliation against authoritative documents, existing systems, and existing UI work.
- **A3:** implementation after explicit approval.
- **A4:** verification and acceptance.
- **A5:** deployment-readiness assessment; deployment remains separately authorized.

## 6. Out of scope during A1

Production application features, production database migrations, real member data ingestion, payment or financial processing, autonomous decision-making, deployment infrastructure changes, and publication.

## 7. Acceptance evidence for A1

A1 is complete only when the assessment, requirements, architecture, security/privacy model, terminology, authoritative organizational hierarchy, risks, dependencies, and open decisions are documented with sources and Owner approval recorded. Until then, A1 remains open.
