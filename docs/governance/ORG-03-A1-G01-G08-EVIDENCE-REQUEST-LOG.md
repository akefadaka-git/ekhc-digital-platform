## 4. Reconciliation protocol on receipt

For every response or document, record and verify:

1. **Issuer:** institution, office, named author/signatory and verifiable origin.
2. **Authority:** constitutional/bylaw, resolution, delegation or policy basis authorizing the issuer to decide or attest.
3. **Date:** issue/signature/effective date, version, expiry and whether superseded.
4. **Scope:** applicable departments, systems, data classes, geographic/organizational boundaries and exclusions.
5. **Approval status:** draft, approved, ratified, adopted, expired, withdrawn or informational; record approval evidence and any conditions.
6. **Integrity and provenance:** secure-channel receipt, original format, signature/seal where applicable, file hash and chain of custody.
7. **Restrictions:** confidentiality, personal data, legal privilege, permitted use, onward sharing and repository publication restrictions.

A public webpage, unsigned email, informal statement, or document without demonstrated authority may be useful context but does not automatically satisfy an institutional evidence requirement.
