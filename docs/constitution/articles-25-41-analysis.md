## 1. Verified text available

The extracted text for Article 25 begins with a definition of **የአጥቢያ ቤተ ክርስቲያናት ኅብረት**. It describes this as the administrative tier immediately following the local church and as a grouping of local churches recognized and assigned responsibility by higher administrative tiers, for coordination of church service under the Church's Word, vision, mission, values, bylaw, and statement of faith.

**System consequence (A1 only):**
- Retain **ኅብረት** as the canonical Amharic label.
- Do not assign an English equivalent in the data model until EKHC's own approved terminology is verified.
- Model it provisionally as a constitutional administrative entity/grouping, not as a translated geographic district.
- Do not infer its exact member churches, boundaries, officeholders, approval workflow, or reporting relationships from this introductory paragraph.

Source location: Sixth Revision, Part III, Article 25, printed page 45 (Scribd text extraction around lines 2005–2019).

## 3. Text-extraction gap

The publicly accessible page extraction exposes the Article 25 introductory definition but does not expose the complete body of Articles 25–41 in a reliable, continuous form. Therefore the following remain **UNVERIFIED / NOT EXTRACTED**:
- full sub-clauses and precise legal wording;
- membership and composition of the General Assembly and Executive Committee;
- election, appointment, eligibility, and term rules;
- delegated powers, decision rights, quorum, and meeting rules;
- office structure and exact staff positions;
- reporting and accountability relationships;
- committee names, mandates, and formation rules;
- distinction between regular, developing, and special forms of ኅብረት, if any, under this Sixth Revision.

No software hierarchy, permission matrix, workflow, or reporting line should be implemented from contents headings alone.
