## 2. Social channels surfaced by the Head Office Telegram channel

The Telegram channel titled **Ethiopian Kale Heywet Church Head Office** describes itself as the official Telegram page and directly publishes these social links:

- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/EKHCHeadoffice
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@heywettv
- Telegram: https://t.me/EthiopianKaleHeywetChurch

The Telegram channel is a useful primary communication stream for public announcements and event evidence. The social links are cross-published by that channel, but should still be recorded as channel claims until independently cross-linked from an EKHC-controlled website or confirmed by the Head Office.

Other EKHC-related channels (for example humanitarian/development commission channels and Hiwot TV) represent distinct entities or brands. Do not treat them as the central Church Head Office account without evidence of governance or ownership.

## 3. Organizational data handling rules

1. Constitutional provisions take precedence over website cards, social posts, and third-party summaries when defining governance, authority, offices, and reporting.
2. Website department names are public-facing labels only until their formal mandate and scope are confirmed.
3. Social posts may establish that an event, announcement, or named office was publicly represented at a particular time; they do not by themselves amend the Constitution or establish a permanent hierarchy.
4. Preserve source date, source URL, publication context, and verification status for every imported organizational fact.
5. Never infer missing offices, titles, committees, levels, geographic boundaries, or reporting lines.
6. Keep Amharic canonical terms where English equivalence is not verified. In particular, retain **ኅብረት** and **ቀጣና** as distinct source terms pending EKHC-approved bilingual terminology.
7. Do not use the prohibited translated label for ኅብረት in UI, schema, API, analytics, or documentation.
