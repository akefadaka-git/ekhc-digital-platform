# A1 Stakeholder and Workflow Discovery

**Status:** Draft discovery baseline — not stakeholder-validated.  
**Purpose:** Convert currently visible EKHC service areas and Owner constraints into a structured assessment plan without assuming constitutional structure, internal reporting lines, or software-domain ownership.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The official EKHC public websites describe a range of ministry and service areas. Their public labels are useful for identifying interview topics and potential user journeys. They do not establish a complete internal organization chart, current mandates, approval authorities, or data ownership.

The workflows below are candidate discovery areas, not approved requirements. No workflow is declared operationally accurate until confirmed by an authorized EKHC stakeholder and, where governance authority is involved, reconciled against the governing documents.

## 2. Candidate workflow discovery matrix

| Discovery area | Candidate workflow to validate | Evidence and questions required |
|---|---|---|
| Organizational administration | Maintain official organizational records and effective-dated changes | What are the authoritative records, who creates/approves changes, and what source defines each relationship? |
| People and membership | Register, update, transfer and verify people or membership records | What are the official categories, minimum data, consent/legal basis, source documents, and correction process? |
| Leadership and assignments | Record appointments, elections, terms, delegations and handovers | Which body has authority for each action, what evidence is required, and how are expiry/revocation handled? |
| Finance | Budget preparation, approvals, receipts, disbursements and financial reporting | Which entities hold funds, approval thresholds, segregation-of-duties rules, chart of accounts and audit obligations apply? |
| Human resources | Personnel records, recruitment, appointments, leave and payroll-related processes | Which records are sensitive, who can access them, what retention rules apply, and what must remain outside the platform? |
| Property and logistics | Asset registration, custody, movement, maintenance and procurement | What is the asset register of record, who approves custody changes, and what supporting documents are required? |
| Missions and local ministry | Plan activities, assign workers, record outcomes and coordinate support | Which activities are reportable, how are outcomes defined, and what information is safe to collect? |
| Theological education | Manage institutions, courses, enrollment, attendance, assessment and certification | Which institutions and credentials are recognized, who validates results, and how are learner records protected? |
| Discipleship and church education | Coordinate learning programs, materials, participation and progress | What programs exist, which age groups are involved, and what safeguarding rules apply? |
| Women, youth and children services | Coordinate programs, events, training and safeguarding-sensitive support | What consent, guardian, safeguarding, access and disclosure rules are mandatory, especially for minors? |
| Social support and community services | Receive requests, assess eligibility, approve assistance and record delivery | What sensitive data is necessary, who decides eligibility, and how are confidentiality and referrals handled? |
| Communication and literature | Prepare, review, approve, publish and archive official communications and documents | Who is authorized to publish, what constitutes an official record, and how are versions and corrections managed? |
| Meetings and decisions | Schedule meetings, manage attendance, record minutes, decisions and follow-up actions | Which meeting types exist, who may convene, quorum/approval rules, signatures and record retention? |
| Reporting and accountability | Submit periodic reports, review exceptions and track corrective actions | What reporting calendar, definitions, approval sequence and audit trail are authoritative? |
| Member-facing services | Provide verified information, requests, announcements and service access | Which services are public, member-only or confidential, and how is identity verified? |

## 3. Cross-cutting workflow requirements to investigate

For each workflow, capture:

- Initiating actor and authorized role/assignment.
- Preconditions and authoritative source records.
- Data fields, classification and minimum-necessary collection.
- Draft, review, approval, rejection, correction and cancellation states.
- Delegation, substitution, escalation and separation of duties.
- Effective dates, expiry, revocation and historical record behavior.
- Attachments, signatures, evidence and document versioning.
- Notifications and communication channels.
- Offline behavior, device constraints and synchronization risks.
- Audit events, retention, export and reporting obligations.
- Accessibility, Amharic terminology and calendar/date behavior.
- Exceptional cases, disputes, appeals and recovery procedures.

## 4. Validation method

For every workflow, obtain an authorized process owner and at least one operational user where appropriate. Review actual forms, registers, sample anonymized records, approved manuals and existing software. Record process maps and decision tables; distinguish formal policy from local practice. Escalate conflicts rather than selecting a convenient interpretation.

Do not collect real member, financial, personnel, child, pastoral-care or other sensitive records for this discovery exercise unless a lawful, approved data-handling process is established. Use synthetic or properly anonymized examples.
