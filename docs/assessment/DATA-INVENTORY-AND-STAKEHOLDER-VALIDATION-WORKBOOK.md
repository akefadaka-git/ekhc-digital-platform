# A1 Data Inventory and Stakeholder Validation Workbook

**Status:** Draft discovery instrument — not validated or Owner-approved  
**Stage:** A1 only  
**Purpose:** Capture candidate information assets and validate their purposes, provenance, custodians, handling needs, and risks with authorized EKHC stakeholders.  
**Use restriction:** Synthetic examples and blank templates only. Do not enter real personal, child-related, pastoral, personnel, safeguarding, identity, or financial records.

## 1. Governance and evidence boundary

This workbook is a discovery aid. It does not authorize collection, import, migration, disclosure, or processing of real records. It does not establish organizational authority, office mandates, record ownership, access rights, retention periods, or a production data model.

Before a workflow or dataset can be validated, reviewers must identify:
- The authorized stakeholder and the basis of their authority to speak for the process.
- The governing policy, procedure, or constitutional provision, including exact edition, article/section, clause, page, language, and source provenance where applicable.
- The actual record or process source, its custodian, and whether it is current and approved.
- The purpose and necessity of each proposed data element.
- Applicable privacy, safeguarding, legal, records-management, and security requirements.

The seventh-edition Constitution remains the required governing source for organizational authority. Current bylaws, approved amendments, policies, and mandates must be separately verified. Public websites and social-media posts may provide leads or corroboration but do not override authoritative instruments. Historical editions are historical context only.

Preserve exact Amharic terminology. Keep ቀጣና and ኅብረት distinct. Do not infer hierarchy, office names, authority, or English translations. No hierarchy or permission model is approved by this workbook.

## 2. Instructions for facilitated discovery

1. Hold a separate session for each workflow or record family where practical.
2. Confirm the participant's role, authority, and scope before recording statements as validated evidence.
3. Ask the process owner to describe the current process from trigger to completion, including exceptions and delegation.
4. Identify records created, received, changed, viewed, shared, exported, archived, and disposed of.
5. Use synthetic values such as “Example Member A” and “Record-001”; never copy live records into this workbook.
6. Record disagreements as unresolved, with each position and its evidence. Do not resolve conflicts by assumption.
7. Separate what is currently done, what is formally authorized, and what is merely desired for the proposed platform.
8. Obtain explicit reviewer confirmation of the session notes; confirmation does not replace Owner approval of the A1 baseline.

## 3. Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Directly supported by a current, authoritative source or confirmed by an authorized process owner within their documented authority; evidence reference recorded |
| PARTIAL | Some relevant evidence exists, but important details or validation remain incomplete |
| MISSING | Required evidence or information has not been supplied or located |
| UNRESOLVED | Conflicting, ambiguous, inaccessible, or unverified evidence prevents a reliable conclusion |
| NO CHANGE | Rechecked evidence remains materially unchanged from the previous recorded review |

Do not use VERIFIED for a public-source lead, an unapproved draft, an inferred relationship, or an unauthorised interview statement.

## 9. Privacy, safeguarding, and security review prompts

For every dataset and workflow, ask:

- Is the purpose specific, necessary, and authorized?
- Could fewer fields or aggregate information achieve the purpose?
- Does the information concern a child or a person seeking confidential care?
- Could disclosure cause physical, spiritual, social, financial, employment, or reputational harm?
- Are family, household, or relationship links necessary and source-verified?
- Could search, reports, exports, notifications, or analytics expose more than the task requires?
- Is the recipient authorized and informed of handling restrictions?
- Are external providers or cross-boundary disclosures involved?
- Could offline copies remain on shared, lost, or compromised devices?
- Can access be revoked and queued changes safely rejected?
- Are correction, complaint, incident response, retention, and disposal processes defined?
- Has a qualified reviewer assessed applicable legal requirements?

Any uncertain child-related, safeguarding, pastoral, or highly sensitive record should be treated as C3 for discovery planning and excluded from demonstrations and test datasets.

## 13. A1 completion criteria for this workbook

- Each candidate workflow has an identified authorized process owner or is explicitly marked MISSING.
- Each confirmed dataset has source provenance, purpose, custodian, data-subject category, and field-level minimization review.
- Provisional classification has been reviewed by authorized stakeholders and remains marked as proposed until approved.
- Child, safeguarding, pastoral, finance, and personnel risks have appropriate qualified review.
- Current legal and policy requirements are evidenced at provision level.
- Retention, correction, disclosure, export, offline, and disposal decisions are source-backed.
- Conflicts and gaps remain visible; no inferred authority or terminology is introduced.
- The source register, risk register, requirements catalogue, and traceability matrix are updated only when evidence actually supports a status change.
- No requirement is marked validated and no baseline is closed without the applicable approval.

## 14. Explicit exclusions

This workbook does not authorize real-data collection, production schemas, migrations, API contracts, role/permission definitions, offline caching, AI processing, external integrations, deployment, signing, publication, A2, or A3.

**Assessment outcome:** PARTIAL — structured discovery instrument prepared; authorized stakeholder evidence, authoritative sources, and Owner approval remain outstanding.
